Suppose the firm’s owners have E million dollars of funds belonging to shareholders (equity) to invest in productive assets, where E = 100 (in the first column). The expected rate of return on capital investment is 10%: that is, the firm expects to make operating profits equal to 10% of the value of the capital.
The firm can also borrow at 5%: the possible amounts of debt, D, are shown in the next column. It can then invest the total, D + E, in new capital.
