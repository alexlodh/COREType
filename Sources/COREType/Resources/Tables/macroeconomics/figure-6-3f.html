After the loan, total assets have increased to 150, since now Marco and Julia each own 50 units of grain, but the value of Julia’s debt to Marco is also 50.
But this is exactly offset by Julia’s liability, so that their collective net worth is unchanged, and is simply equal to the total amount of grain.
Debt cancels out in the calculation of combined wealth.
