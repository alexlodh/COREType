Videos

  • Unit 2: In our ‘Economist in action’ video, John Van Reenen reports on the wide variation in productivity across firms in the economy, and the importance of technological change, good management practices, and competition.
  • Unit 4: Stefanie Stantcheva surveyed 3000 people to find out why inflation makes them angry and what they believe causes it. It is useful to keep in mind the reasoning of ordinary citizens as we build up a model of inflation in this unit.
  • Unit 4: Isabella Weber looks at the rise of ‘sellers inflation’ since 2021. Weber explains how the social context around price increases can make consumers more willing to endure rising prices and less likely to change their spending habits.
  • Unit 8: In our ‘Economist in action’ video, Anat Admati explains the basic principles of equity and debt. Anat explains how banks operate with low equity and create a high risk lending environment. She argues how regulation is essential to control excessive risk and protect against financial crises.
  • Unit 8: This video looks at the lending environment that led to the 2008 financial crisis. It explains how the introduction of leverage and subprime mortgages increased the level of risk in the financial system to an unsustainable level, and why the housing market crashed.
  • Unit 8: In our ‘Economist in action’ video, Claudia Buch, one of the authors of the CORE Insight ‘Too big to fail: lessons from a decade of financial sector reforms’, discusses the effectiveness of regulatory changes prompted by the global financial crisis.
  • Unit 9: In our ‘Economist in Action’ video, Simon Johnson discusses how good institutions influence economic growth.
  • Unit 9: In our ‘Economist in Action’ video, Leonard Wantchekon discusses how the slave trade created mistrust and held back GDP growth in West Africa.
  • Unit 9: In our ‘Economist in Action’ video, Mushtaq Khan explains how Bangladesh surmounted market failures to become a leading garment exporter.
  • Unit 10: In our ‘Economist in action’ video, Esther Duflo explains what happened when it was mandated that randomly selected villages elect a woman to head their local council.
  • Unit 10: James Heckman explores the question of how schooling and preschool experience affects inequality.
